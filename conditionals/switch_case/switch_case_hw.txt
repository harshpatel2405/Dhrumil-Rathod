Question 1 — Smart Energy Billing & Tariff Tier Selection
Difficulty: Medium
Problem Statement:
An electricity distribution board wants to automate its consumer billing system based on the consumer type and monthly power consumption units. The system must process different billing rules for domestic and commercial users, incorporating progressive unit slabs and conditional surcharges.
Requirements:
1. Accept consumer type (1 for Domestic, 2 for Commercial).
2. Based on the consumer type, evaluate the monthly units consumed using tiered brackets.
3. For Domestic users: Tier 1 (0–100 units), Tier 2 (101–300 units), Tier 3 (Above 300 units).
4. For Commercial users: Tier 1 (0–150 units), Tier 2 (151–400 units), Tier 3 (Above 400 units).
5. Apply fixed charges and variable energy rates depending on the selected tier.
6. Include a validation check: If units consumed are negative, display an error message and terminate.
7. Combine with an if-else condition to apply an additional 5% fuel adjustment surcharge only if the total consumption exceeds 500 units regardless of the consumer type.
Inputs: Consumer type choice, units consumed.
Validation: Units cannot be less than 0. Invalid consumer category choice must trigger a default error handler.
Expected Behavior for Invalid Choices: Print "Invalid Consumer Category Selected" and exit.
